    What it does

    Small, fast, and out of the way.

    1. Find any tab in a keystroke

      Ctrl+E, a few letters, Enter. The previous tab is already selected, so Ctrl+E Enter flips straight back. Alt+Z jumps there without opening anything.

    2. Fast and fuzzy

      “gh pr” finds the GitHub pull request. Words can come in any order. The domain ranks above the title, the title above the rest of the URL, and matched letters are highlighted.

    3. Keyboard only, mouse welcome

      Arrows or Ctrl+J Ctrl+K to move, Ctrl+Backspace to close a tab. Prefer the mouse? Click works too.

    4. Private by design

      Zero network requests. Open source, nothing to sign up for, no analytics. What you have open stays in your browser.

    5. Every window, plus more

      Searches all open tabs in all windows, most recently used first. Tabs from another window carry a small window badge. Turn on bookmarks and history (last 90 days) when you want them.

    6. Fits your browser

      Light and dark (Alt+T), a frosted glass panel you can tune: opacity 60 to 100 %, blur 0 to 40 px, size 70 to 150 %, and your own keys.

    Cheat sheet

    Ten keys, and you know it all.

    On macOS, Ctrl becomes Cmd for the open key.

    Privacy

    No network requests. Ever.

    No account, no analytics, no server. Plain JavaScript, no dependencies, about 600 lines you can read in an afternoon.

    tabs
    To list your open tabs and switch between them.
    activeTab
    To show the panel on the page you are looking at.
    scripting
    To put that panel on the page.
    favicon
    To show each site’s icon, read from the browser’s own cache.
    storage
    To remember your settings on your device.
    bookmarks
    Only if you turn on the /b scope. The browser asks you first.
    history
    Only if you turn on the /h scope. The browser asks you first.
    Install

    Four steps, one minute.

    1. Get the code

      Clone the repo: git clone https://github.com/gran-software-solutions/tabnow

    2. Open the extensions page

      Go to chrome://extensions in your browser.

    3. Turn on Developer mode

      It is the switch in the top right corner.

    4. Load unpacked

      Click “Load unpacked” and pick the extension/ folder. Then press Ctrl+E.
